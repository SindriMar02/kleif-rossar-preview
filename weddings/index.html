How many guests can we invite?+

Event and overnight capacities must be confirmed with the host for your plans. This example does not publish an unverified guest limit.

Is the glass pavilion from the Vogue wedding available?+

The pavilion in the published wedding coverage was a temporary installation for that event, not a permanent advertised facility at Kleif. Discuss any structures and event requirements with the host.

Do you offer a wedding package?+

No standard package or price is advertised here. Availability, permissions, suppliers, catering and accommodation need a tailored agreement.
