Kleif Barn

Calmness, rest and recreation.

Kleif Barn combines the old, renovated barn and the rebuilt sheep house that now serves as a modern house for families or friends. It is a perfect blend of the coziness of the living spaces and the playfulness of the Game Barn.

The house offers four en-suite bedrooms, all of them spacious and decorated in a luxurious and cozy way. The villa comes with a kitchen perfect for self catering and a spacious living area with the large panoramic window as its centerpiece. And not to forget the hot tub, with panoramic views of the valley and the farmland. It serves as a luxury base for up to eight people.

The Game Barn

and a cozy corner.

There is direct access to our Game Barn, with a small gym, table tennis, a climbing wall, a small bar and a generous hang-out area. When the houses are rented separately, the Game Barn is private to Kleif Barn.
