In & around Kleif

Follow
your own
rhythm.

A quiet morning at home. A day out in the landscape. A long evening with nowhere else to be. Leave some room in the plan for doing very little.

Eilífsdalur · Kjós · Iceland

01 / Slow days

The art of
staying in.

Read by the panoramic window. Cook with friends. Spend a little longer in the hot tub. Each house has its own private outdoor tub, so a slower day never needs much planning.

02 / Out into the valley

Take the
long way.

The mountains and farmland around Kleif invite you outside. Ask your host about suitable local walks, check the weather and choose a route that fits your experience. Conditions in Iceland can change quickly.

03 / At the table

Good food.
Even better company.

The Farm’s two kitchens make self-catering easy. For a special evening, ask about arranging a private chef. Menus, dates and costs are agreed separately with your host.

A base for exploring

Beyond
the valley.

Kleif feels a world away, yet Reykjavík is roughly 35–40 minutes by car in good conditions. Make the farm your base for days out in southwest Iceland.

Getting here

Good to know

A little
more detail.

Do you run guided activities?+

This page suggests ways to enjoy the area; it is not a list of included tours. Ask the host about local recommendations and any arrangements you would like help with.

Do we need a car?+

A car is recommended for the location and day trips. Allow extra time for weather and road conditions, especially in winter. Check the official road and travel safety services before setting out.

Will we see the northern lights?+

The aurora depends on the season, darkness, weather and solar activity. No sighting is guaranteed, and it is not promised as part of a stay.
