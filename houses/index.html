Good to know

A little
more detail.

Can we book both houses?+

Yes. Kleif Farm and Kleif Barn can be rented separately or together. Tell Erla about your group and preferred dates to explore the right arrangement.

Is this a hotel?+

Kleif offers private villa stays, not individual hotel rooms. Contact the host to discuss the house or houses that best suit your group.

Can you arrange meals?+

The Farm is self-catering, and a private chef can be arranged on request. Discuss menus, availability and costs with the host before your stay.

How do we check availability?+

Send your dates and group details to info@kleif.is. This design example does not display a live calendar or take payment.
