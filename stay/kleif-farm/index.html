Kleif Farm

Warmth, wellbeing and relaxation.

Kleif Farm gives you the luxury of the spacious living area, having the panoramic window as the centerpiece and the fireplace for cozy evenings with your loved ones. The hot tub boasts the most beautiful panoramic views of our valley and farmland.

The house offers five en-suite bedrooms. Three of them are spacious, decorated in a luxurious and cozy way, and two are slightly smaller. The house benefits from a second kitchen, giving the opportunity of catered dinners while still enjoying the privacy of the living area. It serves as a luxury base for up to ten people.
