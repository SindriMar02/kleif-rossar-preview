Good to know

A little
more detail.

Is yoga teaching included?+

No. Kleif has been used for yoga and meditation retreats, but a villa stay does not automatically include a teacher or a programme. Discuss your facilitator and space requirements when enquiring.

Is there a dedicated yoga studio?+

We have not verified a permanent dedicated studio. Ask the host about suitable practice spaces and layouts for your group before confirming a programme.

Can meals be arranged?+

Private chef arrangements are available on request for the Farm. Retreat menus, service, dietary needs, availability and costs must be agreed separately.
