Our story

A farm.
A family.
A feeling.

Kleif has been in our family since the late 1960s. These fields, these mountains and these old buildings are part of who we are.

Come and stayEilífsdalur · Kjós · Iceland

A new life for a familiar place

Old roots.
New memories.

Over the years, we have restored the old farmhouses with care for their history and character. Existing materials have been reused where possible, and traditional details sit comfortably alongside modern design.

It is still the landscape we know and love. Now there is room for your story here, too.

The view that stays with you

Some things
need no changing.

The fields beyond the window. The shape of the mountains. The feeling of coming back inside after a day out. We have made the houses comfortable, but the valley does the rest.

A personal welcome

Meet Erla.

Erla Aðalsteinsdóttir grew up here, on her parents’ farm. Today she welcomes guests to the restored houses and helps shape the details of their stay. If you have a question or an idea, begin with a conversation.
