Our family’s place

A new chapter.The same roots.

Kleif has belonged to our family since the late 1960s. Over the years, hard work and care have shaped the farm into the welcoming place it is today.

In restoring the old farmhouses, we wanted to honour that history. We kept their character, reused materials wherever we could, and gave familiar spaces a new life.

Today, we share the farm with you. The mountains, the slow mornings, the feeling of being a little closer to what matters.
