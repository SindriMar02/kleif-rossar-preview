Retreats

Retreats
at Kleif.

Yoga, writing, wellbeing or simply time to reconnect. A quiet valley, two villas and a Game Barn for the group.

Kleif gives a retreat what it needs: quiet, space and a place to gather. Nine en-suite bedrooms across the two villas, a Game Barn with room to practise together, and a quiet valley of mountains, horses and the river.

The Farm’s second kitchen means meals can be catered while the living room stays yours. Tell us about your group, your programme and your dates, and we will shape the stay around them.
